Two things that could stop B turning into C this time

The court case$604M Dallas verdict (23 Jul 2026) awaiting the judge's decision on entry, then appeal. Insurance renewal early 2027. If costs jump or the judgment stands, part of the step-C profit is eaten before it arrives.
The cost cutsRobinson runs with 35% fewer people than last cycle (11,471 vs 17,717). If it must rehire to handle step-C volume, the profit jump shrinks. Last cycle (2021–22) it added ~2,600 heads.
