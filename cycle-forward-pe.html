What the market pays for CHRW, and how far the multiple can travel

Version 1 (the companion cycle map) values the stock on trailing earnings, which is the right lens for spotting where you are in the earnings cycle. This version uses the blended 12-month forward consensus multiple, the number the market actually trades on. Bands are from the Bloomberg BF P/E screen dated 24 Sep 2026: 5-year average 20.8x, +1 standard deviation 24.9x, −1 standard deviation 16.8x, current 20.9x on roughly $7.15 of forward EPS. Cycle phases shaded as in Version 1.

B · Squeeze, now (2026)
Verdict plus the 2Q margin miss took the multiple from ~28x to 20.9x in eight weeks. The round-trip to the mean is complete; the "compounder" premium is gone.
Exactly on the mean

Takeaway for timing: forward P/E does not reliably mark price bottoms for CHRW, because estimates move as fast as the price. Its value is in sizing how far the multiple can travel from wherever it sits. Today it sits in the middle, which means there is no valuation cushion below and no obvious ceiling above until +1 SD.

Why the bear targets sit where they do

Bernstein applies 21.5x and TD Cowen 21x, essentially the 5-year mean. Their $151 to $152 targets come entirely from 2027 EPS of $7.05 to $7.25 versus the ~$7.45 consensus. At $150 the argument is about earnings revisions, not about whether the multiple is fair.

Why the bull targets need the multiple too

UBS $230 is 28x on $8.20. Jefferies $220 is 28.5x on $7.70. Both sit above +1 SD. So the bull case requires the market to re-award most of the 2025 "compounder" premium, not only for earnings to come through. That is a second bet stacked on the first.

The 2022 lesson

The last time forward EPS was rising into a spread peak, the multiple collapsed from +1 SD to −1 SD in nine months. The GS model's Cyclical Check tab flags exactly this pattern as the risk for 2027: EPS up, stock down, if the market re-labels CHRW a cyclical as spreads peak.

How this changes the buy-versus-wait decision from Version 1

What is unchangedThe cycle sequence, the two tripwires (NAST gross margin and headcount), the five checks on 4 November, and the two breakers (court case, rehiring) are all about earnings. None of them move because the multiple lens changed.
What is sharperBuying at 20.9x means buying at an average multiple. The return has to come from estimates turning up. If they do, history says the multiple drifts back toward +1 SD and you get both legs: roughly $178 to $192 on $7.15 to $7.70. If they do not, the floor is not "it's already cheap"; the floor is −1 SD, about $120 on today's estimates and about $109 if 2027 drifts to $6.50.

Rough payoff at the mean multiple, 12 to 18 months out: bull EPS $8.20 → $171; consensus $7.45 → $155; bear $7.05 → $147; Morgan Stanley $6.07 → $126.
